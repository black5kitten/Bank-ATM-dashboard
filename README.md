# ATM Performance Dashboard (Power BI, Python)

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **ATM Performance Monitoring Dashboard** developed for analyzing ATM operations. The dashboard provides a centralized, analytical view of ATM and card transactions, their market presence, infrastructure of ATMs, operational efficiency of cards, and their digital acceptance, enabling faster decision-making and improved service reliability.

## Objectives

* Provide a consolidated **market presence snapshot** of banking infrastructure including ATMs, POS terminals, Micro ATMs, and Bharat QR deployments.
* Compare **on-site vs off-site ATM deployment** across banks to understand infrastructure distribution.
* Analyze **digital acceptance infrastructure** such as Online POS, Offline POS, Micro ATMs, and Bharat QR by bank.
* Identify leading and lagging banks in infrastructure deployment to support strategic benchmarking.
* Enable quick executive-level monitoring of nationwide banking service reach and accessibility.

## Key Features

* **Infrastructure KPI summary cards** displaying total On-site ATMs, Off-site ATMs, Online POS, Offline POS, Micro ATMs, and Bharat QR deployments.
* **Bank-wise comparison visuals** showing number of On-site and Off-site ATMs across major banks.
* **Digital acceptance analysis** highlighting Online vs Offline POS terminal distribution by bank.
* **Micro ATM deployment insights** identifying banks with the highest financial inclusion reach.
* **Bharat QR adoption analysis** comparing digital payment acceptance infrastructure across banks.
* **Interactive bank filter** enabling focused analysis for individual institutions or comparative benchmarking.
* **Multi-page navigation layout** including Market Presence Snapshot, Infrastructure Deployment, Card Issuance & Usage, and Digital Acceptance sections.

## Data Processing

* Data cleaning and transformation performed using **Power Query**.
* Measures and KPIs created using **DAX** to enable dynamic analytics and filtering.

## Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query (ETL)
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* CSV / Structured operational datasets
* Python, Pandas, Numpy

## Business Impact

This dashboard enables banking leadership, regulators, and infrastructure planning teams to:

* Evaluate **bank-wise infrastructure penetration** and competitive positioning across the country.
* Identify **gaps in ATM and digital acceptance deployment** to guide expansion strategies.
* Support **financial inclusion initiatives** by tracking Micro ATM and Bharat QR rollout across institutions.
* Improve **strategic planning and investment prioritization** using comparative infrastructure analytics.
* Provide a **single executive-ready view** of nationwide banking service accessibility and digital payment readiness.
